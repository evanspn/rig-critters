# Hand-made 16x16 pixel-art critters for rig-critters. Original art (no copied sprites).
# Format (line based, easy to parse from JS too):
#   sprite NAME            starts a sprite
#   pal KEY #rrggbb        palette entry (KEY is one character; '.' is always transparent)
#   16 rows of 16 characters
#   end
# Keys every sprite uses: k outline, b body, d dark body, h light/belly, w eye white, e pupil, l lid (closed eye).
# The eyes are the w and e pixels: when a critter sleeps they are redrawn in the lid color l.
